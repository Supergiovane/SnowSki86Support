Click in, push off, glide.

Support for Snow Ski '86 for iPhone, by Massimo Saccani.

Snow Ski '86 is a calm skiing game in big pixels: ride the gondola, click into your skis and follow 24 snowy pistes down the mountain, through the woods and up to the high peaks, until the last run at sunset.

How to play

  • Touch and hold the left or right half of the screen to turn; let go to run straight.
  • Three stars: reach the bottom, pass every gate and finish without a fall.
  • Ski into the blue circle beside a T-bar to be pulled up the slope.
  • A fall is never the end: the skier gets up and carries on.

Free pistes and the whole mountain

The first three pistes are free. A single one-time purchase opens every piste, for ever: there are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing else to buy. If you reinstall the app or change iPhone, open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchase.

Contact support

For questions or problems, write to maxsupergiovane@icloud.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and the piste number.
